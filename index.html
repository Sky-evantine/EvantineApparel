01 / THE WORLD

A CREATIVE PRACTICE

One person.
Many mediums.

Sky Evantine is a multidisciplinary artist and creative working across music, modeling, visual storytelling, fashion, performance, and design. Sky Mencer is the name behind the work; $oul Fire is the musical project; Evantine is the broader creative brand.

Across each medium, the approach remains personal and observational: finding meaning in movement, atmosphere, style, and the details that shape everyday life.

02 / SOUND

$OUL FIRE / THE MUSIC

Stories with
a pulse.

Under the name $oul Fire, Sky develops music rooted in storytelling, atmosphere, emotional detail, and experimentation. The catalog includes releases such as Mirrors and Violets, Beautiful Rage, Bad News, Snooze, and the Chasing Illusions EP.

MODEL / VISUAL STORYTELLER

Seen in motion.

For Sky, modeling is a study of presence — posture, texture, light, expression, and space. The visual work moves between editorial fashion, natural landscapes, city environments, and quieter, more intimate frames.

Clothing.

Evantine is Sky's independent creative brand, bringing clothing, visual art, objects, ideas, and music into the same conversation.

The brand is intentionally open-ended — a framework for developing a visual and creative language rather than fitting every idea into a single category.
