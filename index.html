01 / THE WORLD

A CREATIVE PRACTICE

One person.
Many mediums.

Sky Evantine is the creative identity of an artist whose work moves between music, modeling, fashion, visual storytelling, performance, and design. Sky Mencer is the person behind the work; $oul Fire is the musical world; Evantine is the larger creative universe.

The thread between them is simple: make things that feel lived-in. Take the long way. Follow the light. Turn experience into something another person can feel.

THE CREATIVE WORLD

Clothing.
Music.
Movement.

Evantine is Sky's independent creative brand: a place where clothing, visual art, objects, ideas, and music can exist together without being forced into one category.

It is less about making a label and more about building a language — one that can change shape as Sky changes.
