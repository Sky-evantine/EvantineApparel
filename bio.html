01 / THE STORY

Sky Evantine is the creative identity of Sky Mencer, an independent artist whose work moves between music, modeling, fashion, image-making, performance, and production. The name Sky Evantine brings those disciplines into a shared creative world, while $oul Fire is the name used for the musical project.

Sky's practice is interested in the details that make a person and a place feel distinct: the way clothing changes movement, the atmosphere of a song, the relationship between a body and a landscape, and the stories people attach to ordinary moments.

Alongside the independent work, Sky has studied and worked in entertainment technology, media, production, and creative environments. CUNY City Tech has featured Sky Mencer in a student profile focused on entertainment technology, stage production, and music.
